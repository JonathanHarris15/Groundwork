A tutor for Obsidian

Learn it from the ground up, inside Obsidian.

Groundwork is a tutor plugin for Obsidian. It maps the concepts you’re learning, marks how well you know each one, and plans the next step toward your goal.

Free plan included. No card needed to start.

See what you actually know

Every goal gets a concept map: a pyramid with the foundations at the bottom and your goals, each marked with a red flag, at the top. The path to the goal is highlighted and off-path concepts fade back. Each concept is marked solid, shaky, learning, rusty, or not started, so you can see where to spend the next hour.

Turn exam material into a plan

Drop in lecture slides, homework, a study guide, or a practice exam. Groundwork pulls out the topics and the depth the exam asks for, then builds an exam goal with a due date. The plan starts with the foundations and works up.

Quizzes that find the edge

Quiz yourself on your own notes and lecture slides. Diagnostic quiz cards test each concept at levels 1 to 5 to find where your understanding stops. A careless slip isn’t counted as a gap, and misconceptions are tracked separately. Groundwork only reads the vault folders you allow.

Flashcards and a tutor, in Obsidian

Flashcard decks are there for quick review of terms and formulas. Reviews count a little; quiz cards set the marks. When you get stuck, chat with the tutor: math renders in LaTeX, answers can use callouts and diagrams, and you can attach slides, PDFs, or images.

Keeps up with you

Every goal has a due date, and the Goals tab shows whether you’re on pace. Mastery fades over time, so rusty concepts come back for review. Progress syncs across your computers.

The path

How it works

  1. 01

    Keep your vault.

    Groundwork plugs into Obsidian. Your notes, links, and folders stay yours.

  2. 02

    Choose what it can see and where it can write.

    Mark folders as context. Mark folders for teaching documents it creates for review later.

  3. 03

    Build on what you know.

    Groundwork tracks concepts and your grasp of them. New lessons grow from that map instead of starting from zero every time.

  4. 04

    Practice until it sticks.

    Quizzes test real understanding, not just recognition, so gaps show up before the exam does.

  5. 05

    Aim at goals and deadlines.

    Set a goal, or feed in exam prep. Groundwork sequences the path: foundations first, then the heaviest-point material when time is short.

  6. 06

    Use your models or ours.

    Start free on Groundwork’s smaller model. Bring your own model for $6/month (the Claude subscription on your computer or a key you paste), or let Groundwork run the models for $20/month.
