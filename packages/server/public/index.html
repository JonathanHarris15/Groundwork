About

What Groundwork is

Groundwork is an AI tutor that lives inside Obsidian. Learning and notetaking stay in one place, on top of the vault you already use.

You point it at folders for context and folders where it can leave teaching notes for later. It builds a map of concepts and how well you know each one, then uses that map as the groundwork for what comes next.

It works across subjects. Ideas from one course or session can show up where they help in another. You set goals and work toward them concept by concept. When exams and due dates matter, it prioritizes the high-weight material first so drop in your prep docs and it will walk you through studying for them.

Bring your own model subscription and API keys, or use Groundwork’s curated set if you want less setup.

  1. 01

    Keep your vault.

  5. 05
